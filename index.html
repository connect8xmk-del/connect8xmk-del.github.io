About A-Z Arcade

A-Z Arcade is a free browser-based collection of mini-games created and developed by the A-Z Arcade Team. The project started as a simple A-to-Z typing challenge and evolved into a collection of games covering typing, memory, reaction, mathematics, puzzles, arcade and trivia.

Our Mission

The goal of this site is to give anyone with a phone or laptop a quick way to sharpen their typing speed, reflexes, memory and general knowledge — no installation, no sign-up, no cost. Every game runs entirely in your browser.

How We're Funded

This site may be supported by advertising to help cover hosting and development costs so the games can stay free for everyone to play.

Your Privacy

A-Z Arcade is designed to minimize data collection. Your scores and level progress are saved locally in your own browser so they're there next time you play, but we do not intentionally collect or store your personal information or gameplay data on our own servers. Third-party services such as Google AdSense may process certain information as described in our Privacy Policy.

How to Play

Getting Started

From the Home screen, scroll through five categories — Skill, Brain, Puzzle, Arcade and Knowledge — and tap any game card to jump straight in. Typing Challenge, Memory Match, Reaction Time, Math Quiz, Sliding Puzzle, Simon Says, Lights Out and Number Tap each also have a "Play 100 Levels" option to work through a structured difficulty ladder instead of free play.

Typing Challenge Modes Explained

Classic asks you to type A to Z in order. Reverse flips it to Z to A. Random shuffles the alphabet so you never know what's next. Speed is Classic mode on a much shorter timer for an extra challenge. Endless has no timer or word limit — you keep typing random letters until you run out of lives, so it's great for pure stamina practice. Word Rush and Sentences replace single letters with full words or sentences, which is closer to real-world typing. Numbers and Symbols train you on digits and punctuation, which most typing games skip but real typing (passwords, code, forms) needs constantly.

Tips to Improve Your Typing Speed

1. Keep your eyes on the screen, not your fingers — glancing down breaks your rhythm. 2. Start on Classic mode with a longer time limit (45-60s) before moving to Speed mode. 3. Accuracy first, speed second — a wrong keystroke costs more time than typing slightly slower. 4. Practice Numbers and Symbols mode separately since they use different finger positions than letters. 5. Use Endless mode for 5-10 minutes a day to build muscle memory rather than one long session. 6. Track your progress in the Statistics screen — small daily improvements in accuracy matter more than occasional fast runs.

🎯 Skill Games

Reaction Time — wait for the box to turn green, then tap it as fast as you can, across 5 rounds; tapping too early restarts the round. Math Quiz — solve as many arithmetic problems as you can before the timer runs out, across Easy, Medium and Hard tiers. Stroop Test — a color name is shown in a different ink color; tap the button matching the INK color, not the word itself. It sounds easy until your brain starts arguing with your eyes.

🧠 Brain Games

Memory Match — flip two cards at a time to find matching pairs; fewer moves and less time means a higher score. Word Search — tap the first letter of a hidden word, then its last letter, to select the whole line. Crossword — fill the 3x3 grid so every row spells a real word, using the clues provided. Simon Says — watch the sequence of colors flash, then tap them back in the exact same order; one mistake ends the round. Lights Out — tapping a light toggles it and its neighbors; turn every light off to win. Wordle — guess the secret 5-letter word in 6 tries; green means the right letter in the right spot, yellow means it's in the word but the wrong spot, and gray means it's not in the word at all. Hangman — guess letters one at a time to reveal the hidden word before you run out of lives. Anagram Scramble — unscramble the jumbled letters into a real word as fast as you can before time runs out.

🧩 Puzzle Games

Sliding Puzzle — tap a tile next to the empty space to slide it, and arrange all the numbers in order. 2048 — swipe in any direction to slide every tile that way; matching numbers merge into their double; try to reach the 2048 tile. Tic-Tac-Toe — get three in a row before the AI does; choose Easy for a relaxed game or Hard for an unbeatable opponent. Number Tap — tap the scattered numbers in ascending order before the timer runs out. Minesweeper — tap a cell to reveal it; a number tells you how many mines are touching that cell. Use the Flag Mode button to mark cells you suspect are mines, then switch back to reveal safely. Clear every non-mine cell to win. Color Flood — pick a color from the palette to flood outward from the top-left corner; get the entire grid to one color within the move limit. Connect Four — tap a column to drop your disc; connect four discs in a row (across, up-down, or diagonal) before the AI does.

🕹️ Arcade Games

Snake — steer with swipe or the on-screen D-pad to eat food and grow longer, without hitting the walls or your own tail. Whack-a-Mole — tap each mole the instant it pops up before it ducks back down. Flappy Tap — tap the screen to flap and glide your bird between the gaps in the pipes. Breakout — drag left and right to move the paddle, keep the ball in play, and smash every brick. Pong — drag up and down to move your paddle and score 5 points before the AI does. Rock Paper Scissors — pick rock, paper or scissors each round and see who the AI throws against you.

🎓 Knowledge

Trivia Quiz — answer 10 random multiple-choice general knowledge questions pulled from a bank of 30, covering geography, science, history and more.

Why Play A-Z Arcade

How to Play A-Z Arcade

Getting started is simple: pick any game card on the Home screen and it opens straight away — no downloads, no account, no waiting. Most games offer a quick Free Play mode plus a structured 100-level ladder you can work through at your own pace, unlocking the next level each time you clear one. Use your keyboard or the on-screen touch controls depending on your device, and check the How to Play & Tips page for mode-by-mode instructions and strategy tips for every game in the arcade.

Benefits of Playing Typing & Brain Games

A few minutes a day on games like Typing Challenge, Memory Match or Math Quiz can measurably improve real-world skills: faster, more accurate typing speeds up everything from schoolwork to job applications; memory games strengthen short-term recall and pattern recognition; reaction-time drills sharpen focus and reflexes; and quick mental-math practice builds number confidence. Unlike passive scrolling, these games give you instant feedback on your own progress, which is one of the fastest ways to actually get better at a skill.

Features of A-Z Arcade

26 free mini-games across five categories — Skill, Brain, Puzzle, Arcade and Knowledge. Eight of them include a full 100-level progression system with increasing difficulty. Everything runs directly in your browser with no installation, and your scores and level progress are saved automatically on your own device so you can pick up where you left off. The site is mobile-friendly with touch controls, swipe gestures and sound effects throughout, and works equally well on phones, tablets and desktops.

Game Guide

A quick-reference sheet for all 26 games — what each one asks of you, how to control it, how scoring works, and one tip to get better fast.

🎯 Skill

Typing Challenge — Objective: type the given letters, numbers, symbols, words or sentences correctly before time runs out, across 9 different modes. Controls: your device keyboard, or the on-screen text box on mobile. Scoring: points per correct character plus a combo bonus for consecutive correct hits; accuracy is tracked separately from speed. Tip: slow down by 10% and your accuracy — and therefore your real score — usually goes up more than it goes down.

Reaction Time — Objective: tap the box the instant it turns green, across 5 rounds. Controls: tap/click anywhere on the box. Scoring: your reaction time in milliseconds for each round, averaged at the end; tapping before it turns green restarts that round. Tip: rest your finger lightly on the screen rather than hovering — the extra travel time adds up over 5 rounds.

Math Quiz — Objective: solve as many arithmetic problems as possible before the timer ends, at Easy, Medium or Hard difficulty. Controls: number pad input plus a GO button. Scoring: points per correct answer, with a streak bonus for consecutive correct answers. Tip: at Hard difficulty, division problems are pre-generated to divide evenly — if your answer isn't a whole number, you've made an arithmetic slip, not found an edge case.

Stroop Test — Objective: tap the button that matches the INK COLOR a word is printed in, not the word itself. Controls: tap one of the four color-name buttons. Scoring: one point per correct match within the time limit. Tip: read the color, not the letters — try softly squinting so the word shape blurs and only the color stands out.

🧠 Brain

Memory Match — Objective: flip two cards at a time and find every matching pair. Controls: tap any face-down card. Scoring: fewer moves and less time both push your score higher; difficulty (Easy/Medium/Hard) sets how many pairs are in play. Tip: on your first few flips, deliberately explore new cards instead of re-checking ones you've already seen — you learn the board faster that way.

Word Search — Objective: find all 8 hidden words in the letter grid. Controls: tap the first letter of a word, then its last letter, in a straight line (any direction, including diagonal and backwards). Scoring: a completion timer; no penalty for wrong selections. Tip: scan for double letters or unusual letters (Q, X, Z) first — they narrow down a word's location fastest.

Crossword — Objective: fill the 3x3 grid so every row forms a real word, guided by the clues. Controls: tap a cell and type a letter. Scoring: pass/fail on Check Answers; incorrect letters are highlighted so you can fix just those. Tip: since the grid is symmetric, a letter you're sure of in a row also tells you a letter in the matching column.

Simon Says — Objective: watch a sequence of colors flash, then repeat it back in the same order. Controls: tap the four colored panels. Scoring: level-based — the sequence gets one step longer and slightly faster each level. Tip: say the color names in your head as they flash; verbal encoding holds a sequence in memory longer than visual memory alone.

Lights Out — Objective: turn every light off. Controls: tap a light to toggle it and its up/down/left/right neighbors. Scoring: level-based, with a move limit that gets tighter as the grid grows. Tip: work row by row from the top — once a row is fully off, only touch cells in the row below it so you don't undo your progress.

Wordle — Objective: guess the secret 5-letter word within 6 tries. Controls: type a 5-letter guess and tap GO. Scoring: green = correct letter, correct spot; yellow = correct letter, wrong spot; gray = not in the word. Tip: your first guess should use 5 different common vowels/consonants (like CRANE or SLATE) to gather the most information before you start narrowing down.

Hangman — Objective: guess the hidden word letter by letter before you run out of lives. Controls: tap letters on the on-screen A-Z keyboard. Scoring: pass/fail — you have 6 wrong guesses before the word is revealed and the round ends. Tip: guess common vowels (E, A) and consonants (R, T, N) first; save rare letters like Q and Z for when you've narrowed down the word's shape.

Anagram Scramble — Objective: unscramble jumbled letters back into the original word, as many times as possible before time runs out. Controls: type your answer and tap GO (or press Enter). Scoring: one point per correct word within the 60-second timer. Tip: look for common word endings (-ING, -ED, -ER) in the scrambled letters first — spotting a likely suffix shrinks the puzzle fast.

🧩 Puzzle

Sliding Puzzle — Objective: slide numbered tiles around a single empty space until they're in order. Controls: tap any tile adjacent to the empty space to slide it. Scoring: level-based, tracked by number of moves; the grid grows from 3x3 to 5x5 at higher levels. Tip: solve the top row and left column first and treat them as "locked" — then the rest reduces to a smaller version of the same puzzle.

2048 — Objective: slide and merge numbered tiles until you reach the 2048 tile. Controls: swipe in any direction, or use the on-screen arrow pad. Scoring: points equal to the value of every merge; your best tile is also tracked. Tip: pick one corner and always build your highest tile there — avoid swiping in the direction that would move it away from that corner.

Tic-Tac-Toe — Objective: get three of your marks in a row before the AI does. Controls: tap any empty cell. Scoring: win/draw/loss record is tracked across games; Easy AI plays randomly, Hard AI plays a perfect, unbeatable strategy. Tip: against Hard mode, the best you can achieve is a draw — taking the center on your first move gives you the best odds of forcing one.

Number Tap — Objective: tap scattered numbers in ascending order before time runs out. Controls: tap the numbers directly on the grid. Scoring: level-based; both the amount of numbers and the time limit scale with level. Tip: locate 1 and 2 first before you start tapping — knowing where the sequence begins prevents wasted eye movement mid-run.

Minesweeper — Objective: reveal every safe cell without clicking on a mine. Controls: tap a cell to reveal it; toggle Flag Mode to mark cells you suspect are mines instead of revealing them. Scoring: pass/fail — a single mine ends the round instantly. Tip: start by revealing corners and edges; they touch fewer hidden cells, so they're statistically safer opening moves.

Color Flood — Objective: turn the entire grid into a single color, starting from the top-left corner, within the move limit. Controls: tap a color swatch to flood outward from your current region into that color. Scoring: pass/fail based on whether you finish within the move limit; fewer moves used means a cleaner win. Tip: look for the color that borders your current region in the most places — flooding into it captures the largest new area per move.

Connect Four — Objective: connect four of your discs in a row — horizontal, vertical or diagonal — before the AI does. Controls: tap a column to drop your disc into the lowest open slot. Scoring: win/draw/loss record tracked across games. Tip: controlling the center column gives you the most possible four-in-a-row lines, so contest it early rather than playing only on the edges.

🕹️ Arcade

Snake — Objective: eat food to grow as long as possible without hitting a wall or your own tail. Controls: swipe on the canvas, or use the on-screen D-pad. Scoring: one point per food eaten; your length is your live score. Tip: hug the outer walls in a loop when the board gets crowded — it's easier to control than cutting through open middle space.

Whack-a-Mole — Objective: tap moles the instant they pop up, within 30 seconds. Controls: tap a hole when a mole appears. Scoring: one point per successful whack; moles appear faster as your score climbs. Tip: keep your eyes on the center of the grid rather than tracking each hole individually — peripheral vision catches the pop-up faster than direct focus does.

Flappy Tap — Objective: fly your bird between an endless series of pipe gaps without hitting one. Controls: tap the screen to flap upward. Scoring: one point per pipe successfully passed. Tip: use short, repeated taps to hover through a gap rather than one big flap — fine control beats big corrections in a game this fast.

Breakout — Objective: bounce the ball with your paddle to break every brick on the board. Controls: drag left/right on the canvas to move the paddle. Scoring: 10 points per brick destroyed; you have 3 lives before the game ends. Tip: hitting the ball with the edge of the paddle sends it off at a sharper angle — aim for the edges to reach bricks tucked into corners.

Pong — Objective: score 5 points against the AI before it scores 5 against you. Controls: drag up/down on the canvas to move your paddle. Scoring: first to 5 points wins. Tip: hitting the ball with the edge of your paddle changes its angle more sharply — use that to aim shots away from the AI's current position.

Rock Paper Scissors — Objective: beat the AI's random pick across as many rounds as you'd like. Controls: tap Rock, Paper or Scissors. Scoring: your wins, draws and losses are tallied live. Tip: there's no pattern to exploit — the AI picks randomly each round, so past rounds never affect what comes next.

🎓 Knowledge

Trivia Quiz — Objective: answer 10 random multiple-choice questions correctly. Controls: tap your chosen answer. Scoring: one point per correct answer out of 10, drawn from a bank of 30 general-knowledge questions across geography, science, history and more. Tip: eliminate the two most obviously wrong options first — it turns a 4-way guess into a coin flip when you're unsure.
